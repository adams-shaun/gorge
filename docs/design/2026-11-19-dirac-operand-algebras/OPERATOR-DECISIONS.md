# Operator decisions for the operand-algebra design

Base: origin/main `9ce12ae3fb`. DESIGN.md beside this file gives the measurements. The **recommended answer comes
first**; the alternative and its cost follow. These items are protected: a seat or agent may not change them without
your sign-off. That covers `maxStateBytes` and the State bar tests, `scripts/dirac-gate.sh`, the evidence constants in
`dirac/oracle/evidence.go`, the golden chain heads and the wire goldens.
